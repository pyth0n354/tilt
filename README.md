# Tilt

A seasons mod for Minecraft that changes how the world *behaves*, not just how it looks.

> **Status: in development.** Nothing is released yet.

Existing seasons mods tint the foliage and slow your crops down. Tilt does something different:
crops belong to **seasons** rather than growing more slowly, biomes carry their own **fixed
seasonal identity**, and villagers trade what their **biome and season** can actually produce.

## Planned features

- **Seasonal crops, not slower crops.** Winter changes *what* you farm, never how long you wait.
- **Biomes have seasonal identities.** Cherry Grove is eternally spring, Dappled Forest eternally
  autumn, snowy biomes eternally winter. Only temperate biomes cycle.
- **Greenhouses.** Grow out of season under glass — skylight without direct sky access.
- **Hemispheres.** North and south run opposite seasons; the equator barely changes.
- **Biome-typed villager trades.** Vanilla gives villagers seven biome appearances and no
  behavioural difference. Tilt makes them trade what they can actually grow.
- **Seasonal fishing, freezing, and weather.**

No new blocks. No new items. No HUD. You learn the season by looking at the world.

## Building

Requires **JDK 25**.

```
./gradlew build
```
